# 素材 02 · 卧室夜景氛围图 ×3

## 用途
品牌展示页「四项核心能力」区的视差背景图。文字(暖白大字)会直接压在图上,所以**暗部必须干净、构图要留出文字区**。

## 统一规格
- **尺寸**:2400 × 1500(16:10)
- **格式**:JPG(质量 85+)
- **色调**:深靛夜色为主(`#0A0C1E` ~ `#241F4B` 区间),暖光点缀(`#F5DFAE` 系的灯光)
- **构图**:画面一侧(左或右 1/3)保持大面积暗部留白,供文字排版
- **禁止**:人物面孔、杂乱物品、过曝光源、冷白荧光灯

## 通用负面提示词(每张都加)
```
people, face, clutter, bright daylight, cold fluorescent light, overexposed, cartoon, text, watermark
```

## 逐张提示词

### 1. 床头暖灯(对应「主动调节」)→ 交付名 `bedroom-lamp.jpg`
```
Cozy bedroom at night, warm dim bedside lamp glowing softly, deep indigo shadows filling most of the frame, left third of frame in clean darkness, cinematic photography, serene, high-end hotel mood, muted warm tones, shallow depth of field, no people
```

### 2. 窗外月色(对应「睡眠档案 / 一夜的数据」)→ 交付名 `bedroom-window-moon.jpg`
```
Bedroom window at night with soft moonlight streaming in, sheer curtain gently glowing, moon visible in deep indigo night sky, right third of frame in clean darkness, cinematic photography, quiet and serene, muted warm and indigo tones, no people
```

### 3. LED 氛围微光(对应「自然语言指令」)→ 交付名 `bedroom-led.jpg`
```
Modern bedroom at night with a soft warm LED light strip glowing along the headboard, gentle amber ambient light, deep indigo shadows, left third of frame in clean darkness, cinematic photography, premium and calm, no people
```

## 交付
放入 `public/assets/scenes/`,文件名如上。

## 验收标准
- 三张色温一致(暖光 + 深靛),放在一起像同一组片子
- 每张都有 1/3 干净暗部,白字压上去可读
- 情绪是「安静高级」,不是「样板间广告」
